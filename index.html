What it is

Your safety plan, before work starts.

MS2 Risk Check is a phone-and-tablet app built on the MS2 safety program. Pick the activities, tools, and equipment for a job, and the app assigns each one an MS2 risk level using Troy King's field-built risk assessments and SOPs: 112 activities across 20 trades, and 49 tools and pieces of equipment.

It starts from OSHA minimums, and you add your site's own rules on top. The crew gets what it needs before the first tool comes out: a risk report, permits and inspections, a training plan, and an inspection sheet.

How it works

  1. Name the job.One task for today's crew, or a whole project broken into phases.
  2. Pick the work, one trade at a time."Will this job include Confined Space work?" Yes or no. If yes, tap the types.
  3. Pick tools and equipment.The app suggests what the MS2 SOPs call for.
  4. Answer yes or no.Public nearby? Could it stop production? Recent injuries? Your answers can only raise a risk, never lower it.
  5. Get your plan.Risk report, permits, life-saving controls, a training plan, an inspection sheet, and AHAs.

What is Make Safety Make Sense about?

Make Safety Make Sense provides construction safety consulting, training, and full-time project safety professionals for manufacturers and the contractors who work on their sites. We show you how to keep your people safe while identifying high-risk activities and cost-effective ways to manage them.

What are Profit Parasites?

Profit Parasites are policies and procedures that cost you money and add nothing to safety or to your work. In some cases, they're simply wasting money. We've identified 20 that we see most often. One of the most common is going beyond the standard to "make it safer." There is only safe or not safe. Anything past safe is wasted time and money.

What are Profit Preservers?

We use the term two ways. First, a process that can be revised to save you money is a Profit Preserver. Second, we use it to recognize the people in the profit preservation fields: safety, legal, human resources, and others.

Do you work outside your home area?

Yes. We serve clients nationwide, both for consulting and for on-site training.

Can individuals sign up for OSHA 30?

Yes. Our OSHA 30 Hour classes are regularly scheduled and open to individual enrollment. OSHA 10 is taught at your site for your crew. See Upcoming Classes on the Training page, or contact us to reserve a seat.

Can you train our crews at our site?

Yes. We deliver OSHA 10 and 30, and customized training such as confined space, excavation, and scaffolding, at your facility, built around your hazards.

What is the MS2 Risk Check app?

A phone-and-tablet app, sold as a yearly subscription, that turns the MS2 safety program into simple yes/no questions and produces a risk report, permits, a training plan, and an inspection sheet for each job. The Safety Manager add-on also builds AHAs for RFP submittals. Subscribers get discounted rates on customized consulting. Learn more.
